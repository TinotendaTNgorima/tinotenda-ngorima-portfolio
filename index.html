About me

I work at the intersection of engineering, information systems and data-driven decision-making.

Tinotenda Ngorima is an Industrial and Manufacturing Engineering graduate from Harare Institute of Technology and a Master of Science in Information Technology candidate at American National University.

My professional interests include business information systems analysis, decision modelling, service-oriented architecture, incident management, predictive analytics, customer relationship management and machine-learning approaches to cybersecurity.

My work focuses on applying engineering principles, analytical methods and digital technologies to complex operational challenges. My long-term academic interests include doctoral study in Industrial Engineering, Engineering Management or a related interdisciplinary field.
